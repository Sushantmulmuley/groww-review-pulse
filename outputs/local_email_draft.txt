Fee explanation (reusable support snippet)

## What to know about charges deducted on a trade
- Brokerage is ₹20 or 0.1% of the order value, whichever is lower, with a minimum of ₹5 per executed order.
- Selling shares from the demat account also attracts a DP charge: ₹16.50 charged by Groww (not charged when the debit value is under ₹100) plus ₹3.50 charged by the depository.
- 18% GST applies on brokerage and DP charges.
- Regulatory and statutory charges such as STT, stamp duty and SEBI turnover fees are listed separately on the pricing page.
- Intraday orders carry no DP charge.

Sources: [Groww: Brokerage charges and pricing](https://groww.in/pricing); [Groww Help: How much does Groww charge for stocks?](https://groww.in/help/stocks,-f&o,-ipo-&-mtf/sx-demat-account/how-much-does-groww-charge-for-stocks-1)
